Small Claims Copilot

A free web app that turns a California small claims dispute's scattered documents into a hearing-ready packet that separates what you claim from what your documents prove, and shows what's wrong first.

Behavior

Problems first: conflicts side by side (neither silently chosen) and must-resolve gaps. Then follow-ups with why it matters and, where relevant, a hand-picked official California Courts link with a last-checked date. Then backed / your-word-only statements, a dated timeline (undated events listed as "date unknown"), the evidence list, and a summary built only from labeled statements and backed facts.

Acceptance criteria
  • Demo: $2,400 vs $2,800 conflict at the top with both excerpts.
  • Location "no/not sure" → gap + follow-up linking an official CA Courts page.
  • Clicking ✓ backed reveals document + excerpt that really appears in it.
  • Unsupported statements show ⚠ your word only, never as fact.
  • Missing dates/amounts show "unknown," never invented.
  • A document saying "ignore previous instructions…" is treated as content; nothing changes.

Print packet   Clear case
Behavior

Edit the summary, statement wording, and follow-up answers. ✓ backed only ever comes from an excerpt. Print gives a clean packet with statuses as text. Clear case wipes everything after a confirmation.

Acceptance criteria
  • No control marks a statement backed without an excerpt.
  • Printed packet has no buttons/tabs; includes notice, excerpts, statuses.
  • Clear case removes description, answers, documents, packet.

Look and feel

Clean like a bank app, using the palette and type from lazare.herzi.ai without its terminal theme. Geist for UI; Geist Mono for excerpts, so proof reads as quoted record. Rounded cards. Status always shown as icon + label, never color alone. No AI-app gradients, sparkles, or chat bubbles.
